Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 June 2026

Pickly ("we", "us", "the app") helps you compare grocery prices across Australian supermarkets. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how it's handled.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, and a securely hashed password (we never store your password in plain text).
  • App activity: your shopping list, saved favorites, and price alerts, so the app can show them to you across sessions.
  • We do not collect: your location, contacts, photos, or any payment information. Pickly does not process payments.

How we use this information

Solely to operate the app's core features: logging you in, syncing your shopping list and favorites, and sending price-alert notifications you asked for. We do not use your data for advertising.

Sharing your information

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Data is stored on our hosting provider's servers solely to run the app.

Data retention & deletion

Your data is kept while your account is active. To request deletion of your account and all associated data, email us at the address below.

Children's privacy

Pickly is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
